In August I wrote about running Claude Code on DeepSeek V4 Flash, and the most useful part of that post was DeepSeek's own config: V4 Pro for the real work, Flash only for search and subagents. On September 10 DeepSeek shipped V4.1 Flash and rewrote that advice. Flash now goes in every slot.

Going through the release, the finding worth your time is a quiet one. DeepSeek's endpoint remaps Claude model names, so picking Opus in Claude Code sends you to V4 Pro, the older, slower and pricier model. Images get through the endpoint, but PDFs do not.

If you use DeepSeek with Claude Code, or you copied my August setup, this is the update. It also covers what the new pricing means for anyone working Indian hours, when the peak rate applies to most of the morning.
